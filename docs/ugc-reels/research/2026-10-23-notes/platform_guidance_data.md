# Platform guidance and large-sample data on short-form video ad creative (Meta, TikTok, 2024-2026)

Scope note: researched 22 Oct 2026. Meta's own facebook.com/business pages do not render for the fetch tool (they come back as just "Facebook"), so several Meta figures below are taken from search-engine summaries of the Meta page or from secondary write-ups. Each item says which. TikTok's help-center pages did render and are quoted first-hand. Items dated before 2024 are marked **[OLDER]**.

## Meta: creative best practices for Reels ads (hooks, brand timing, safe zones, captions, sound, Performance 5, Advantage+ creative, Andromeda and creative diversity)

### Takeaway
Meta's official line is a three-part "creative essentials" set for Reels: 9:16 full-screen vertical video, audio on, and key elements inside the safe zone. Meta reports a 34.5% lower cost per result for that set versus image ads on Reels, from a meta-analysis of 15 split tests. Since Andromeda (rolled out Dec 2024 to Oct 2025), Meta's systems can handle far more ads per person, and Meta's guidance moved from narrow targeting to *creative diversification*: different concepts, formats, motivations and personas, not cosmetic edits. On brand timing Meta's own guidance has two sides: its help-center best practices say to put brand and key message in the first 3 seconds, while secondary sources attribute "hook-first" results to Meta. That conflict is unresolved.

### Cited Findings
**Reels creative essentials and measured result (Meta, official)**
- Meta's Reels ads page reports that Reels ads built with 9:16 video, audio, and key creative elements in the safe zone had a **34.5% lower cost per result than image ads on Reels**. Meta's footnote calls this a **meta-analysis of 15 split tests** (Meta-run, not independent). Taken from a search summary of the page, because the page itself would not render. — [Meta for Business: Reels ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads)
- The same Meta page says Advantage+ creative tools can automatically expand creative to the 9:16 Reels aspect ratio. Taken from a search summary. — [Meta for Business: Reels ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads)
- Secondary write-ups attribute to Meta a figure of **16% better cost per result and 13% higher ROAS**. It applies when Reels ads that had the creative essentials *plus at least one extra element* (a hook in the first few seconds, human presence, text overlay, or lo-fi content) were added to business-as-usual campaigns. No primary Meta URL was found, so treat it as unverified. — [Benly Reels guide](https://benly.ai/learn/meta-ads/meta-ads-reels-ads-guide), [AdLibrary Meta creative best practices](https://adlibrary.com/posts/meta-ad-creative-best-practices)
- An agency playbook attributes another figure to Meta: 9:16 video with audio gives about **7% lower CPA when it makes up at least 20% of an ad set**. It also recommends that each ad set hold a 9:16 video with audio, a 4:5 or 1:1 video, and a 4:5 or 1:1 image. Secondary source; not verified against Meta. — [Growth Collective Meta Creative Playbook](https://www.wearegrowth.co/blog/meta-creative-playbook)
- A Q2 2025 deck titled "Creative Diversification Playbook for Reels", apparently from Meta, is posted unofficially on Scribd. It stresses 9:16 video with audio and a "holistic creative strategy" to maximise campaign "liquidity". Its origin could not be confirmed. — [Scribd upload](https://www.scribd.com/document/907158170/Screenshot-2025-06-06-at-1-28-41-PM)

**Brand and hook timing (conflicting)**
- Meta's Business Help Center best practices reportedly say to feature brand and key message **within the first 3 seconds**, because early branding helps recall. This comes through a secondary search summary; the primary page could not be fetched. — [search summary citing Meta Help Center; see CreativelySquared](https://www.creativelysquared.com/article/hook-first-brand-second-the-new-reality-of-social-media-advertising)
- Contradicting that, an agency article says Meta tested hook-led ads against brand-led ads and found **23% higher completion and 17% stronger recall**. Another blog cites a "Meta 2025 Creative Best Practices report" for **up to 89% higher completion** when the hook comes before front-loaded branding. Neither gives a Meta URL, so both are unverified and should not go in a playbook as fact. — [CreativelySquared](https://www.creativelysquared.com/article/hook-first-brand-second-the-new-reality-of-social-media-advertising); [AdLibrary](https://adlibrary.com/posts/meta-ad-creative-best-practices)

**Captions and sound**
- Meta lists sound and captions as recommended (not required) for Reels and Stories. Per one guide, Meta does not support automatic captions on Facebook Reels ads, so captions should be burned into the video. Secondary source. — [AdLibrary Meta Ads Creative Best Practices](https://adlibrary.com/posts/meta-ads-creative-best-practices)
- A secondary article says Meta's best-practice docs recommend captions on all Reels ads, with no URL given. — [AdLibrary Reels Ads 2026](https://adlibrary.com/posts/reels-ads)
- No reliable Meta-published "share of Reels watched with sound on" figure was found in 2024-2026 sources. Practitioner sources split between leading with an audio event in the first half second and designing for sound-off first. — [AdLibrary Reels Ads 2026](https://adlibrary.com/posts/reels-ads); [Benly Reels guide](https://benly.ai/learn/meta-ads/meta-ads-reels-ads-guide)
- Scale context **[OLDER, 2023 newsroom via secondary]**: more than 140 billion daily Facebook Reels plays and more than 200 billion Instagram Reels plays, attributed to Meta's newsroom. — [AdLibrary citing Meta](https://adlibrary.com/posts/reels-ads) → [Meta newsroom](https://www.facebook.com/business/news/insights/reels-monetization-update)

**Andromeda and creative diversity (Meta primary plus secondary)**
- Meta Engineering (2 Dec 2024): Andromeda is Meta's ads *retrieval* stage. It narrows tens of millions of ad candidates to a few thousand per person before ranking. Meta reports **+6% recall** and **+8% ads quality on selected segments**, plus a 10,000x increase in model capacity. — [Meta Engineering blog](https://engineering.fb.com/2024/12/02/production-engineering/meta-andromeda-advantage-automation-next-gen-personalized-ads-retrieval-engine/)
- The same post says the number of eligible ads is growing with Advantage+ automation and generative-AI creative tools, and that Andromeda was built to scale with that growth. Figures in the post:
  - Advertisers new to Advantage+ who turned on its AI targeting saw a **22% ROAS increase**.
  - Businesses using image generation see an estimated **+7% conversions**.
  - More than **1 million advertisers made 15M+ ads in one month** with Meta's generative-AI tools.

  — [Meta Engineering blog](https://engineering.fb.com/2024/12/02/production-engineering/meta-andromeda-advantage-automation-next-gen-personalized-ads-retrieval-engine/)
- Meta's advertiser guidance on diversity, quoted second-hand, describes diversity along three axes: **formats, customer motivations, personas**. It says "increased ad diversity can improve people's experience with ads and drive better advertiser outcomes." The same analysis notes that Meta never says Andromeda measures how similar creatives are, so the claim that it "merges near-duplicates" goes beyond what Meta published. — [Soku: Meta Andromeda explained](https://soku.ai/blog/meta-andromeda)
- Rollout timing per secondary sources: deployed December 2024, global rollout across accounts complete by about October 2025. — [Confect](https://confect.io/tactics/meta-andromeda-2026); [MTM Agency](https://themtmagency.com/blog/meta-andromeda-october-2025-update-why-creative-diversity-now-defines-ad-performance)
- A figure of "8-15 ads per ad set" attributed to Meta internal guidance could **not** be verified. — [search summary of agency sources; see Wonderful](https://www.usewonderful.com/blog/meta-andromeda-creative-strategies)
- Agency consensus: distinct concepts (testimonial, demo, story, humour) beat small edits of one ad, and broad targeting with Advantage+ placements makes creative the main lever. — [Dentsu](https://www.dentsu.com/ae/en/our-latest-thinking/meta-andromeda-strategy); [AdScale](https://adscale.com/blog/meta-andromeda-update/)

**Performance 5**
- Performance 5 is Meta's framework covering account simplification, Advantage+ automation, creative diversification, Conversions API, and results validation. Secondary summaries say that diversifying messages, visuals and formats lets the system match each ad to each person. One cites **32% better efficiency** with diverse assets, without naming a primary source. No live Meta page defining it was found. — [Overt Digital Marketing](https://overtdigitalmarketing.com.au/meta-performance-5-framework/); [Benly](https://benly.ai/learn/meta-ads/meta-ads-performance-5-framework)

**Creator / partnership ads (Meta-sourced numbers)**
- Meta's long-quoted partnership-ads figures: **19% lower CPA** and **3.9% lower cost per purchase** versus comparable formats. These are repeated by vendors; original study details were not found. — [AdNabu](https://blog.adnabu.com/facebook-ads/meta-partnership-ads/)
- A Meta white paper (reported dated 7 Oct 2026) gives a **45% lower median cost per incremental purchase** for partnership ads. It comes from **20 conversion-lift studies by EMEA advertisers, 1 Jan to 30 Jun 2025, at 92% confidence** (below the usual 95%). It is company-supplied. — [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/meta/meta-partnership-ads-30-percent-45-lower-incremental-cost/)
- An independent long-term view: an IPA analysis (Oct 2025) as reported indexes creators at **99 for short-term ROI and 151 for long-term**, with a **3.35 long-term multiplier versus 1.98 for paid social**. — [Relevant Audience citing IPA](https://www.relevantaudience.com/meta/meta-partnership-ads-30-percent-45-lower-incremental-cost/)

### Inferences
- For a script playbook, the safe Meta rules are: shoot native 9:16, include audio, burn in captions, and keep text and CTA inside the safe zone. Those are the only elements backed by a Meta-published test (34.5%, 15 split tests).
- On "brand in the first 3 seconds" versus "hook first", the evidence is weak on both sides. A workable rule is to open on the hook, *with the product or brand visible in frame* within 3 seconds, without a logo card. That fits both lines of guidance.
- Andromeda means a batch of scripts should differ in *angle, persona and format*, not just the first line. Several distinct concepts per product is more useful than many hook swaps of one video.

### Gaps
- Meta's primary pages (Reels ads page, Help Center "text overlays and safe zone for Stories and Reels" article 980593475366490, Creative Shop) could not be fetched directly. Exact wording and footnotes need a manual check in a browser.
- No primary Meta source was found for the 16%/13%, 23%/17%, 89%, 7%, 32% or "8-15 ads" figures.
- No Meta 2024-2026 figure was found for the share of Reels watched with sound on.

## TikTok: Creative Center / creative best practices, first-3-seconds data, sound, native/creator style, Spark Ads, TikTok Shop

### Takeaway
TikTok's current help-center guidance (updated June 2025) says:
- **Timing:** state the proposition in the first 3 seconds, hook within 6 seconds.
- **Format:** 9:16, 720p or higher, sound or music on.
- **Text:** 5-10 words per second.
- **Structure:** hook, then selling points, then a clear CTA.
- **Look:** creators, employees or customers, with a less polished DIY look.
- **Volume:** 3-5 creatives per ad group, 3-5 diversified ad groups, with big differences between creatives.

TikTok's own measured lifts (length 21-34 s, captions, multi-scene, voiceover) come from a December 2021 model study and are **older**.

### Cited Findings
**Current official guidance (first-hand)**
- "Creative best practices for performance ads", last updated **June 2025**:
  - State the content proposition **within the first 3 seconds** for recall and awareness.
  - Prioritise the hook **within the first 6 seconds** (suspense, surprise, emotion).
  - Vertical **9:16**, at least **720p**, use sound or music, keep content inside the UI safe zone.
  - On-screen text at **5-10 words per second**; captions or overlays for context.
  - Structure: **hook, USPs, clear CTA**; transitions, stickers and graphics to hold attention.
  - Feature **creators, employees or customers**; a **DIY or less polished** look; trends, memes, challenges.
  - **3-5 creatives per ad group** and **3-5 diversified ad groups per campaign**; "test creatives with big differences" during exploration.
  - Refresh when delivery declines, adding to the existing ad group.
  - The page cites no statistics.

  — [TikTok Ads Help: Creative best practices for performance ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/creative-best-practices)
- "Best practices for Video Shopping Ads with TikTok Shop", updated **March 2026**:
  - At least **3-5 creatives per ad group**.
  - Add a new creative **about once a week** or at signs of fatigue (falling CTR or engagement).
  - The creative must match the advertised product(s); stress limited-time Shop promotions.
  - Product Anchor Link is the default CTA "because of its historically strong conversions and click-through rates".
  - The page does not cover hook or length.

  — [TikTok Ads Help: Video Shopping Ads best practices](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/best-practices-for-video-shopping-ads-with-tiktok-shop)
- Product GMV Max best practices (updated about June 2026): more videos with product anchors give more GMV potential; link more accounts and add Spark Ads posts to the Creative Library. Taken from a search summary. — [TikTok Ads Help: Product GMV Max](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/best-practices-for-product-gmv-max)
- A third-party guide claims TikTok Seller guidance asks for a hook of 3 seconds or less, the product in use, on-screen price or value text, and an explicit "tap to shop" CTA. It was not verified against TikTok. — [Tolstoy](https://gotolstoy.com/blog/tiktok-shop-shoppable-videos-guide)

**TikTok's measured creative lifts [OLDER: Dec 2021]**
- From a machine-learning model over "thousands of ads" in TikTok's auction, validated across regions (no exact sample or period given). Each figure is a conversion lift:

  | Creative choice | Compared with | Conversion lift |
  |---|---|---|
  | 720p or higher | Lower resolution | 312% |
  | 9:16 full screen | Black bars | 91% |
  | Clear text CTA | No stated next step | 152% |
  | 21-34 s length | Shorter or longer | 280% |
  | Ecommerce: multiple scenes | One person, one continuous shot | 38% |
  | Ecommerce: captions or text showing offer or CTA | No text or CTA | 80% |
  | Ecommerce: human voiceover plus written offer | Without this pair | 87% (did not hold in Europe or Brazil) |
  | Gaming: text in the first 7 s | Text later | 43% |

  The post also says length is flexible. — [TikTok blog: Triple-digit conversion lift (21 Dec 2021)](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/creative-that-drives-conversions)
- Related TikTok figures repeated by partners, primary date unclear and likely 2021-22 **[OLDER]**:
  - 1 in 4 top videos is 21-34 s, with a 1.6% impressions lift.
  - 9:16 videos get 60.5% more impressions.
  - 93% of top-performing TikToks use audio.
  - Varied scenes give a 40.6% impressions lift.

  — [Marin Software summary](https://www.marinsoftware.com/blog/how-to-nail-your-tiktok-ad-creative)
- TikTok's own guidance says **over 63% of the highest-CTR videos show their key message or product in the first 3 seconds** ("our research shows"; no sample, date or method). Often misquoted as a general hook statistic. **[OLDER, about 2021-22]** — [search summary of TikTok guidance; see Hansen Commerce](https://hansencommerce.com/insights-tiktok-hook-3-seconds)

**Sound [OLDER: 2021]**
- A Kantar study for TikTok (blog dated 8 Jun 2021; no sample size or markets given) found:
  - **88%** of TikTok users say sound is essential to their TikTok experience.
  - **73%** would "stop and look" at TikTok ads with audio.
  - TikTok was the only platform tested where ads with audio raised both purchase intent and brand favourability (no figures given).

  TikTok's advice is to build sound-first, using the free Commercial Music Library. — [TikTok blog: Kantar sound report](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/blog/kantar-report-how-brands-are-making-noise-and-driving-impact-with-sound-on-tiktok)
- Several widely repeated figures, such as a "71% decide in 3 seconds" and "68% better recall", could not be traced to TikTok. — [search review; e.g. TikAdTools](https://tikadtools.com/blog/tiktok-ads-creative/)

**Creative Codes**
- "Creative Codes" is TikTok's framework: hook in the first 3 seconds, native production, design for sound-on. This comes only through a secondary summary; the original TikTok Creative Codes page was not fetched. — [Stackmatix Creative Center guide](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/tiktok-creative-center-guide)

### Inferences
- TikTok's two timing rules can be used together: *what it is or what it does for you* by second 3, and *the reason to keep watching* by second 6.
- The 5-10 words-per-second caption rate is the only official caption-pace figure found on either platform. It is a usable script constraint: about 15-30 words of on-screen text in a 3 s hook at most, and fewer is safer for non-native readers.
- 21-34 s is TikTok's strongest length datapoint, but it dates from 2021 and is model-derived. Treat it as a starting range, not a rule.

### Gaps
- No 2024-2026 TikTok-published lift numbers were found for length, hooks, sound or creators. The numeric evidence is all 2021-22.
- No first-hand TikTok Spark Ads performance statistic was found in this pass.
- Creative Center "Top Ads" insight pages were not fetched; they mostly require a login and filters.

## Independent large-sample creative data (Motion, Varos, Triple Whale, Northbeam, others): hook rate, hold rate, length, UGC vs studio

### Takeaway
The largest independent dataset found is Motion's (now runneth.com) Creative Benchmarks 2026: 550k+ Meta ads, 6,000+ advertisers, about $1.3B spend, Sep 2025 to early Jan 2026. It shows strong spend concentration: about 5% of ads are "winners" and about half get almost no spend. UGC has a 7.56% winner hit rate against 6.97% for high-production. Text-only (11.6%) and "newness" hooks (11.37%) top the hit-rate lists. Hook and hold-rate "benchmarks" circulate widely but are practitioner ranges, not platform standards.

### Cited Findings
- **Motion Creative Benchmarks 2026:**

  - **Spend concentration:** about 5% of ads are winners; about half get no or little spend; about 6% of ads take the majority of an account's spend.

  - **Hold rate** (Motion definition) = 15-second plays ÷ 3-second plays.
  - Definitions of hold rate vary between sources, and Meta publishes no official hook-rate benchmark.

  — [Sepia Lab hook-rate benchmarks](https://sepia-lab.com/en/blog/hook-rate-benchmarks); [Motion: key creative metrics](https://motionapp.com/blog/key-creative-performance-metrics); [AdLibrary hold rate](https://adlibrary.com/posts/hold-rate)
- AdSights (2025 data) reports median thumbstop of **about 36% for warm retargeting (range 30-45%)** and **about 22% for cold prospecting (range 18-28%)**. - One source claims TikTok retention runs 5-10 points below Meta on identical creative. Unverified. — [search summary; Jetfuel](https://jetfuel.agency/creative-performance-metrics-for-dtc-video-ads-hold-rate-thumbstop-and-thruplay-benchmarks-2026/)

### Inferences
- For DTJ's playbook, Motion's data supports:
  - UGC and founder styles as a core format.
  - Offer-first, urgency and "new" hooks as high hit-rate openers.
  - High creative volume, because winners are rare (about 5%) and spend concentrates on them.
- Hook-rate targets of 25%+ cold and 30-35%+ "good" are usable as internal review thresholds. They must be labelled practitioner benchmarks, not Meta or TikTok numbers.

### Gaps
- Varos, Triple Whale and Northbeam creative benchmark numbers were not reached in this pass; no figures from them are reported here.
- No independent large-sample study comparing *video length* or *UGC vs studio CPA/ROAS* (as opposed to hit rate) was found for 2024-2026.
- India-specific creative benchmarks were not found.

## Safe zones and caption placement for 9:16 Reels and TikTok

### Takeaway
For Meta Reels, the widely repeated guidance keeps text and logos out of the **top ~14%, the bottom ~35%, and ~6% on each side**. On 1080x1920 that is about 269 px top, 672 px bottom and 65 px each side. Leave the **bottom 40%** clear if there is a disclaimer. For TikTok, the official answer is downloadable safe-zone templates in Ads Manager. A third-party tool attributes **240 px top, 660 px bottom, 120 px sides** on 1080x1920 to TikTok, plus a right-side action-button column. All of these figures are secondary and should be checked against the live templates.

### Cited Findings
- **Meta Reels:**
  - Clear zones: top 14%, bottom 35%, 6% each side (about 269 / 672 / 65 px on 1080x1920, leaving about 950x979 px of safe area).
  - Recommended upload size: 1440x2560.
  - Bottom 40% clear when a disclaimer is shown, attributed to the Business Help Center.

  — [Kive Meta ad sizes](https://kive.ai/learn/meta-ad-sizes); [AdNabu Meta safe zones](https://blog.adnabu.com/meta-ads/meta-safe-zones/); [Blip safe zones](https://withblip.com/blog/how-to-use-meta-ad-safe-zones-without-cutting-off-your-creative/)
- Conflict: some guides give the bottom margin as 20-35% rather than a flat 35%. — [search summary, Cinerads](https://www.cinerads.com/blog/meta-ads-video-creative-best-practices)
- Meta's own Help Center article on text overlays and safe zones for Stories and Reels exists (ID 980593475366490), but its content could not be fetched. — [Meta Business Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/980593475366490)
- On paid Reels, Meta's CTA button sits over the lower area, and the like / comment / share icons sit on the right. Keep the CTA text and key copy clear of the lower-right corner. — [Strike Social safe-zone guide](https://strikesocial.com/blog/maximize-ad-visibility-and-cut-through-the-noise-with-safe-zone-guides); [Kive](https://kive.ai/learn/meta-ad-sizes)
- **TikTok:**
  - The official help page says to keep content in the UI safe zone "per ad specifications", without giving numbers. — [TikTok Ads Help](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/creative-best-practices)
  - One source says TikTok publishes downloadable safe-zone files rather than fixed pixels, because the area varies with ratio and caption length. — [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/tiktok-ads/tiktok-video-size-ad-specs/)
  - A third-party checker attributes to TikTok Ads Manager Help: **240 px top, 660 px bottom, 120 px each side** on 1080x1920, plus an action-button column on the right. — [Upload-Post safe-zone checker](https://www.upload-post.com/tools/safe-zone-checker/)
- Caption placement advice:
  - About 5-7 words per line, in the lower third but above the CTA overlay. — [AdLibrary](https://adlibrary.com/posts/meta-ads-creative-best-practices)
  - Preview with platform captions turned on before export. — [House of Marketers](https://www.houseofmarketers.com/guide-to-safe-zones-tiktok-facebook-instagram-stories)

### Inferences
- One master safe area works for both platforms. Inside the 1080x1920 frame, keep text between about 270 px from the top and about 670 px from the bottom, at least about 120 px from each side, and clear of the right-hand icon column. Burned-in captions then sit about 60-68% down the frame. This is consistent with DTJ's existing "bottom mark ~69%" caption rule, which sits at the lower edge of the safe area.

### Gaps
- No first-hand Meta or TikTok page with exact 2026 safe-zone numbers could be fetched. Download the official templates (Meta Ads Manager guardrails, TikTok Ads Manager safe-zone files) to confirm before locking a template.
