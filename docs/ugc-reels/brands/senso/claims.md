# Senso Foods: products and what a reel may say (21 Oct 2026)

Sources: Senso's own pages, read 21 Oct 2026: the range page https://sensofoods.com/india/ready-to-make-ocp-india/, the 16
product pages under https://sensofoods.com/my-product/<slug>/, and https://sensofoods.com/about-us/. Turmeric + black pepper:
Shoba et al., Planta Medica 1998 (https://www.thieme-connect.de/products/all/doi/10.1055/s-2006-957450).
Harsh's decision (21 Oct 2026): reels may make flavour-specific benefit claims, e.g. what turmeric in chai does.
Each claim below is marked:
- **[Senso]**: Senso's own words on its site.
- **[Ingredient]**: a widely known property of an ingredient that is in the pack.
- **[Do not]**: unsupported, or the kind of line that gets a Meta health ad rejected.

## The brand (for founder, social-proof and "who makes this" hooks)
- Senso Foods Pvt. Ltd., Rajkot, Gujarat. Started 2009 as APEX Industries with the instant-premix brand "Ready4You"; renamed
  SENSO in 2013; Pvt Ltd in 2014. Founders: Nauman Charaniya (MD) and Yatin Charaniya (Director). [Senso]
- "Over 2 million sachets every day", "over 2 million cups every day", "more than 30 countries" (one older paragraph on the same
  page still says 16 countries: confirm with Senso before a reel uses a number). [Senso]
- Airline supplier: "Qatar Airways, Oman Air, Salam Air, Air India, Star Air". Strong hook ("the chai you had at 35,000 feet"),
  but naming an airline in an ad needs Senso's written confirmation; until then say "served on international airlines". [Senso]
- Awards: Brand of the Year 2018 and 2019 (Silicon India); Gulfood 2019 Most Innovative Hot Beverage for the Turmeric Latte;
  Gulfood 2020 (Vegan Chai Tea Latte Capsules: the history says finalist, the award list says winner, so say "finalist");
  FSSC 22000 v6 (SGS); Star Export House; International Glory Award 2025. [Senso]
- Blue Tea's best runner is "25 lakh customers, no celebrity, no TV ad". Senso's honest version of that hook is the scale and
  the award ("2 million cups a day", "the turmeric latte that won at Gulfood Dubai"), not invented customer counts.

## Every product shares (the site's "Why choose this?" list) [Senso]
Instant: one sachet + hot water, stir, ready ("a hot cup of chai in less than 2 minutes", Masala sachet page); travel-friendly
single sachets; gluten free; no preservatives; easily soluble.
- Careful with "100% natural" and "no artificial flavours". The site says both, but some ingredient lists include maltodextrin,
  E551, hydrogenated fat, glucose syrup or "natural identical flavoring substance". Use them only for products whose list is
  plain (milk solids, sugar, tea, spices).

## Product by product
Pack = sachet size x count. Ingredients exactly as listed on Senso's page.

### Turmeric Latte Mix with Saffron ("Golden Milk", "Haldi Doodh")
- Pack: 20 g x 6. Hot (150 ml hot water) or cold (120 ml milk/water, ice, blend).
- Ingredients: milk solids, sugar, turmeric, saffron, black pepper.
- Award: Gulfood 2019 Most Innovative Hot Beverage. [Senso]
- May say:
  - "haldi doodh without the haldi stains / without boiling" [Senso: instant]
  - "turmeric, saffron (kesar) and black pepper" [Senso]
  - "caffeine free, a swap for your evening tea or coffee" [Senso]
  - "black pepper is in there for a reason: it helps your body absorb turmeric's curcumin" [Ingredient]
  - "the same haldi doodh your nani gave you when you had a cold" (a memory, not a cure) [Ingredient/tradition]
  - "warm, soothing" [Senso]
  - "Senso says it supports immunity" (the site says "boosts your immunity" and "Immune booster") [Senso]
- Do not:
  - say "cures", "treats" or "prevents" a cold or infection
  - say "anti-inflammatory" as a medical effect (the site says black pepper adds "anti-inflammatory quality": keep it to
    "black pepper with turmeric, the classic combo")
  - give the "2000%" absorption number (one small 1998 study; later work did not repeat it)
  - say "low sugar" unless the box in hand says so (the earlier DTJ page was for a low-sugar box)

### Detox Green Tea Kahwa Instant Mix
- Pack: 22 g x 10 on the site (the box from the senso-01 reel says 3 g x 10: two different packs, so check the one in hand). Hot water only.
- Ingredients: maltodextrin, rock salt, green tea extract, citric acid, extracts of ginger, black pepper, clove, cinnamon, basil
  (tulsi) and cardamom, asafoetida (hing), anti-caking agent E551.
- May say:
  - "Kashmiri-style kahwa in 10 seconds, not 10 minutes of boiling" [Senso: instant]
  - "green tea with ginger, clove, cinnamon, tulsi, cardamom, black pepper and a pinch of hing" [Senso]
  - "zero sugar" (the nutrition table shows 0 g sugar per 3 g) [Senso]
  - "the after-a-heavy-meal cup" and "helps with a bloated stomach" (the site says it is "Beneficial in Bloated Stomach") [Senso]
  - "ginger and hing, the classic remedies Indian homes reach for after a heavy meal" [Ingredient/tradition]
- Do not:
  - say "detox" as a body effect ("flushes toxins"); "Detox" is fine as part of the product's name
  - claim weight or fat loss (unlike Blue Tea, Senso never claims it)
  - promise it treats cough, cold, stress or anxiety (the site claims these; Meta rejects them)

### Instant Hot Chocolate (25 g x 6)

    ask Senso first

## Faults on Senso's own site (tell Senso; never copy into an ad)
- Every product page also carries the Plain Tea Premix text block (a template leftover), so read only the first block.
- Nutrition tables are mixed up across pages (e.g. Plain Tea shows "1010 g" carbohydrate, and the Hot Chocolate table carries tea
  values). Never quote a nutrition number from the site except the kahwa's 0 g sugar; use the box in hand.
- "Belgium Chocolate" vs the ingredient list; "100% natural" vs maltodextrin/E551; 16 vs 30 countries.
