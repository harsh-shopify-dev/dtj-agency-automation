# DTJ acquisition scripts: fact-check and 10/10 rewrite (12 Oct 2026)

Source: `C:\Users\harsh\Downloads\DTJ Client Acquisition Points.xlsx` (9 scripts). Workbook with every column: `2026-10-12-dtj-acquisition-scripts-refined.xlsx` (sheets: Refined scripts, Fact check, Psychology).

Format of each script: a 3-second Gujarati sticker → one problem → one calculation the founder can check himself (the Aha) → the structural fix → one indifferent qualifier + universal CTA. 35–45 seconds spoken. `[__]` = DTJ must fill in.

## Psychology (what changed in tone)

- Desperation leaks: 'amne direct contact karo', 'amari team no contact karo', 'jo tamne pan … joitu hoy' — an operator qualifies, a salesman invites. Every script now ends with one indifferent qualifier ('Ame darek brand sathe kaam nathi karta…').
- Self-labels instead of proof: 'Ame marketing agency nathi, ame growth partners chiye', 'Top brands aam nathi karti' — every agency says this. Replaced with a calculation the founder can check on his own screen.
- Jargon still leaking: D2C, vanity metrics, hard numbers, tech stack, algorithm, scale, valuation, independent website, load time. Replaced with chopdo, munim, hisaab, dhara, kaani dol, vechan, nafo.
- Hooks were English sentences. The first 3 seconds now carry a Gujarati sticker with a rupee number or a loss.
- Sales ≠ profit: two scripts called the dashboard number 'nafo'. A dhandho founder spots that instantly, and it kills credibility. It's now the Aha in script 4.
- Each script now has exactly one 'hisaab' line the founder can verify himself (settlement gap, ₹40 per ₹1,000, ₹170 → ₹230, ₹714 → ₹357, ₹170 vs ₹1, 180 vs 2 days). That is what earns respect from this audience: not claims, arithmetic.

## 1. Amazon/Flipkart: fee gai, nafo kya?  (original: 6/10)

3. Settlement report kholo. 'Product sales' ane 'Net proceeds' vacche no farak — e Amazon no sacho bhaag che.  
   *[AHA: Seller Central settlement view, highlight the two columns and the gap]*
4. Ane grahak? E Amazon no che. Hajar order vechya, pan ek pan grahak no number tamari pase nathi.  
   *[Order detail page with buyer contact hidden]*

   *[Logo + digitaltusharjoshi.com]*

## 2. Payment fees: order aave e pehla j 4% gaya  (original: 5/10)

**What was wrong:** Math is muddled: hook says '5% of net profit', body says '4.5% margin' — those are different things. '+18% GST' won't land: GST-registered businesses claim GST on fees back as input credit, and a Gujarati trader knows it. True numbers: Razorpay 2% (UPI too), Shopify Basic adds 2% on third-party gateways (Grow 1%, Advanced 0.6%), not on COD. The stronger 'Aha' is the Basic-vs-Grow break-even, which most founders have never calculated.

**Sticker (0–3 s):** Dar ₹1,000 na order par ₹40 — khali payment levama

1. Tamari website par ₹1,000 no prepaid order aave, to ₹40 to paisa levama j jaay che.  
   *[Calculator: 1,000 × 4% = 40]*
2. Razorpay no 2% — UPI par pan. Ane Shopify Basic plan par bijo 2%, karan ke India ma Shopify nu potanu payment nathi.  
   *[Razorpay pricing page ('2%'), Shopify India pricing ('2% third-party fee'). Small text: 'Razorpay May 2026, Shopify Oct 2026']*
3. Mahine ₹10 lakh nu prepaid vechan? To ₹40,000 khali fee. Varshe ₹4.8 lakh.  
   *[AHA 1: calculator 10,00,000 × 4% = 40,000 × 12]*
4. Have game juo: Shopify no Grow plan mahine ₹4,100 vadhare, pan fee 2% thi 1%. Mahine ₹4.1 lakh thi vadhare prepaid vechan hoy, to Basic plan par rehvu sidhu nuksaan che.  
   *[AHA 2: break-even on screen: ₹4,100 ÷ 1% = ₹4.1 lakh]*
5. Ame ads chalavta pehla aa hisaab sarkho kariye chiye — plan, gateway, COD charge. Aa bachat sidhi nafa ma jaay.  
   *[Shopify admin → Plan page]*
6. Ame darek brand sathe kaam nathi karta. Tamaru online vechan mahine ₹[__] lakh thi upar hoy, to digitaltusharjoshi.com par jao — athva niche aapeli link juo.  
   *[Logo + digitaltusharjoshi.com]*

**Aha:** Break-even: Grow costs ₹4,100/month more than Basic but saves 1% → above ₹4.1 lakh/month of prepaid sales, Basic is a loss.

**Sources (show small on screen):** Shopify India pricing (read 12 Oct 2026): Basic ₹1,499, Grow ₹5,599 (yearly billing); third-party fee Basic 2% / Grow 1% / Advanced 0.6%; not charged on COD/manual payments. Razorpay pricing ('Last updated May 2026'): 2% incl. UPI platform fee, 18% GST on the fee.

**Confirm before running:** That DTJ sets up plans/gateways as part of store work. Amounts exclude GST (recoverable for GST-registered sellers); say 'GST alag' if asked.

## 8. Fix fee nahi — pehla hisaab, pachi fee  (original: 5/10)
