## Do not copy

- **Fake countdowns and fake scarcity.** "Offer expires in 00h 29m" restarts on every visit; "Last 15 spots left". Inventing
  urgency breaks our "never fabricate" rule and costs trust.
- **₹99 entry fees and bonus stacks "worth ₹10,000".** That is a different business model (paid webinar). Only if Tushar
  decides to run a paid or live session.
- **Very long pages with 10+ video testimonials.** Our audience reads less; we keep the swipe rows.
- **Hype words** ("No-BS", "Total autopilot", "10X").

## Suggested order for our pages

1. Sticky phone CTA bar (1) + facts row under the hero (5) + "we review every request" line (9). No new facts needed.
2. Three-step form (8). No new facts needed; same fields.
3. Today vs After block (4) replacing Symptoms + What we fix. No new facts needed.
4. Then → Now case cards with category filter (2, 3) once Harsh confirms each brand's category.
5. Show the audit (7) and who's behind it (11) once Harsh sends a sample audit and Tushar's photo/bio.

## What I noticed (the patterns that matter most for DTJ)

1. **The founder is the brand.** WebVeda puts Ankur Warikoo in the hero; Rahul Malodia, Rajiv Talreja, Siddharth Rajsekar,
   GetNos and Yuvraj Juneja all show the founder's face, name and numbers; two use a short founder video. DTJ is literally
   "Digital Tushar Joshi" and showed no face anywhere → built: `FounderBand` on every variant.
2. **The winners say who it's for, out loud.** "For D2C founders doing ₹20L+/month", "Register only if you spend ₹1L+/month".
   This filters before DTJ's team spends time (DTJ checks every lead). Needs DTJ's real minimums.
3. **The offer is something you can count or picture.** "First 3 profit fixes", "personalised roadmap" (with a picture of it),
   "₹10,000 of bonuses". DTJ's "free growth audit" is abstract → built: "What your free audit shows you" (3 items + drawing).
   A real sample audit image would make it concrete.
4. **Rupees before → after, tagged by category.** Rahul ("₹10Cr → ₹50Cr · Manufacturing · 1 year"), Yuvraj
   ("Pet care · ROAS 2.3x → 3.9x"). Our ResultCards already show ₹ before → after; adding the category needs Harsh.
5. **The form starts small.** Hormozi asks one thing per screen; the agencies that qualify ask spend and revenue as choices.
   → built: 5-step form on `/audit`, taps first, contact last, same fields.
6. **Always-visible button on phones.** Every event page and GetNos → built: `StickyCta` on every variant.
7. **Urgency is mostly fake** (timers that restart, "last 15 spots"). We don't copy it.
8. **Length:** Indian funnels run 8,000–36,000 px. Ours stay ~6,100–7,200 px with all the proof.

## The better acquisition point (research summary)

Ranked (opinion based on the evidence; most numbers are vendor-published, see links):

1. **Same free audit, asked one question per screen, taps first** (built as `/audit`). Uses only DTJ's current promise.
   Evidence: multi-step enquiry form 0.96% → 8.1% ([Venture Harbour](https://ventureharbour.com/multi-step-lead-forms-get-300-conversions/), self-reported);
   cutting 9 → 6 fields *lowered* leads 14%, rewording labels raised them 19% ([Aagaard / Unbounce](https://unbounce.com/cta-conf/Michael_Aagaard_CTAConf2015.pdf)):
   keep the qualifying questions, make them easier.
2. **"Where are your sales leaking?" quiz** that ends in the audit request: quiz starters → leads ~40% (platform average,
   [Interact](https://www.tryinteract.com/blog/quiz-conversion-rate-report/)). An on-screen result is a new deliverable → DTJ decides the scoring.
3. **Click-to-WhatsApp ads with screening questions** as a second ad route (not a button on the page: no exits). Meta's India
   case: Learnwise 4X more qualified leads with WhatsApp Flows ([WhatsApp Business](https://whatsappbusiness.com/resources/success-stories/learnwise/)). New channel, fees, reply speed → DTJ decides.

Whatever wins: reply fast. Leads contacted within an hour were ~7x more likely to qualify than an hour later
([HBR 2011, as quoted](https://www.smartcompany.com.au/marketing/20110704-are-you-letting-online-leads-go-cold/)).
Not now: paid audit (new price), calendar booking (clashes with "we check first"), Meta instant forms (cheaper leads, weaker quality).
