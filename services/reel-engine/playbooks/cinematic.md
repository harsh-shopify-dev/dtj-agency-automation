# Cinematic reel playbook

- A short film, not a talking head: 5 to 9 shots, each with one clear idea (establishing wide, detail close-up, the product hero).
- Copy the reference's light and colour exactly (golden hour, diya glow, cool morning) and keep it the same across shots.
- Camera moves are slow and simple: push-in, slide, tilt, or a still frame. One move per shot.
- The product is the hero of at least two shots, still and sharp, label readable; never opened, flipped or moved inside a clip.
  If a clip breaks, a slow push-in on the approved still is a good fallback.
- Words are few: a voice-over line or on-screen text per 2-3 shots, never more than 3 words a second.
- People (family, hands) are natural: hands joined to wrists, faces that do not morph, no more people than the scene needs.
- Festival reels: real festival details (diyas, rangoli, sweets) placed around the product, not covering it.
- Music carries the edit: cuts on the beat; only licensed tracks (Pixabay Content License, not "Content ID registered").
