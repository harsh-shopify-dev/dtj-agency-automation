# Founder reel playbook

- The founder speaks to the camera like a person, not an ad: why they started, what they do differently, one proof.
- Every claim is in the brand's facts.md: no invented numbers, awards, customers or results.
- The founder is a NEW character described in words unless the brand gives its own founder footage; never copy a real person's face.
- Setting: the real workplace (kitchen, workshop, packing table, farm), with the product and its making visible.
- Hook in the first 2 s: a strong first sentence ("We almost shut down in 2021...") only if it is a fact the brand gave.
- B-roll shots of the product or the making between talking shots; the product stays still and the same inside a clip.
- At most 3 words a second; 8 s talking clips; captions at the bottom mark.
