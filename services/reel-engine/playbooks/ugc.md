# UGC reel playbook (from DTJ's runs: book-01, senso-01, Tapri, Royal Dosa; docs/ugc-reels/)

- Looks like a real person filmed it on a phone: handheld selfie, propped phone or a friend holding it. Say which in every video prompt.
- Hook in the first 2 s: a face and a reason to keep watching (a problem, a surprise, the product in hand).
- A new character described in words (age, look, clothes, room, light). Never a real creator's face from a reference.
- Lines: at most 3 words a second; 8 s clips by default (6 s minimum), never 4 s for a spoken line.
- One product state per clip. The product never opens, flips or moves inside a clip; it stays still and the same.
- Hands joined to visible wrists or sleeves; no extra or melted fingers.
- Real objects the team names (the exact kettle, the ribbed cutting-chai glass) must appear as named.
- Describe the product exactly as the pack is (e.g. Royal Dosa Paper is a flat, thin, crispy triangle sheet like khakhra, NOT a folded dosa).
- No AI-ad words in prompts (cinematic, 8k, ultra-detailed, studio, flawless).
