## 1. The book in one paragraph
Debut novel (first printing 2020, Paper Publication). Mumbai, 2018. Sagar and Malar were born two years apart in the same
building, Ganesh Bhuvan Housing Society, Malad East; the building's terrace is their "adda". Their circle grows to four:
Harsh (Sagar's childhood friend, back after four years) and Sai (who moves into the building). A road trip to Hampi changes
all four. Harsh is being forced by his father into a marriage with Khushboo from a rich Surat family; Harsh and Malar fall in
love; the friends (with Khushboo herself) plan how to get Harsh out of the wedding. Themes: friendship as chosen family,
body image and illness, family and "samaj" pressure, finding a purpose. The epigraph: "become someone's Hanuman".

## 2. Characters

**Sagar** (narrator figure, born 1996, B-wing). Romantic, searching for purpose. Loved a girl in college, took "five years to
move on", then fell for Devaki, who "rejected me plainly"; Malar says he sent Devaki a love letter "way too early" (p.2-3).
Writes "all his internal questions" in a diary (p.92). Watches the Padma Shri awards and feels empty about his own purpose.
Is moved by a girl playing dhol-tasha at Ramleela Maidan, who turns out to be Sai (p.92, p.117). Gives Malar the
"You Are Beautiful" sticker (p.65). The book is framed as his diaries.

**Malar** (born 1994, A-wing). Topped her school with 96%, chose Commerce, wanted to be a Chartered Accountant "and become
financially independent like her mother" (front chapters). Her father died when she was five. Has hypothyroidism (Dr. Meera's
clinic, p.20): weight gain (70 kg by March 2018), tiredness, stretch marks, dark patches. With Nimit for "seven years" (p.3);
Nimit pulls away as her body changes; his father comments on her weight; Malar ends it on self-respect ("Main aapke baap ke
paise ka nahi khati", p.43). Starts the gym with Harsh; falls in love with him; proposes to him first on the hill in Hampi.

**Harsh** (22, plywood business, p.48-49). Cricket player; "the six-pack and the muscles that most guys just dream about";
posts shirtless gym videos and "romantic shayaris on his Instagram stories". Has agoraphobia: "the fear of crossing roads ...
No matter how empty the roads are" (p.105); Malar takes his hand to cross. Dated Aayushi (they said "I love you" in two days on
Instagram, p.53); his father secretly drove her family away and lied to him. His father arranges his marriage to Khushboo from
Surat, "the richest in our samaj" (p.60); later the family drugs and confines him before the wedding; after the marriage he
files an FIR against his father (epilogue).

**Sai** (from Kochi, Kerala; moved to Bengaluru after 12th for Hotel Management, p.112). "Bangalore's pace took a toll on her."
Plays dhol-tasha; wants to start a travel company "and take everyone to Hampi" (p.125); guides the friends through Hampi's
history. Sagar literally falls onto her on the terrace the first time they meet. Tells Sagar they do not need a label.

**Khushboo** (Surat; the fiancée Harsh's father picked). Offers Harsh a cold "arrangement" at first, then becomes the one who
plans the rescue (sangeet dance as cover, p.219-220). Had to give up someone she loved herself.

**Others:** Devaki (Sagar's crush, rejects him), Nimit (Malar's ex of seven years), Aayushi (Harsh's ex), Dr. Meera (Malar's
doctor; the Serena Williams pep talk; the "20 rupees" bananas line), Harsh's father and mother, Malar's mother.
Names NOT in the book must never be used (check with `book_search.py --names`).

## 3. Places and timeline
- Ganesh Bhuvan Housing Society, Malad East, Mumbai; the terrace ("their adda"); WhatsApp group "Terrace Buddies" (p.130).
- Dr. Meera's clinic; the gym.
- The road trip: Mumbai to Hospet (hotel Malligi) to Hampi: Vitthala Temple and its stone chariot (p.153), sunset at
  Malayavantha Parvata (p.154), sunrise at Matanga Hill (p.170), a 300-year-old flowering tree.
- Surat: the wedding; the sangeet the night before.
- Story present: 15 November 2018; flashbacks to 2013 and earlier.

## 6. Claims
Allowed (official site sagarchudesara.com, read 20 Oct 2026): "National Bestseller"; "2,00,000+ copies sold"; every order
comes with a free hand-signed letter from the author and a free bookmark; buy from the official website.
Do not use: the rating (the site shows "4.9 from 24,661+ reviews" and "4.9 from 2,000+ readers" on different pages: ask which
is right first); the price (changes); "the author replies to every reader" unless re-checked; any review or reader quote that
does not exist; anything a character did that is not in the book. The speaker reacts to the story; she does not invent a
personal history ("I was in a reading slump", "I cried all night") or results.

## 7. Reserved and sensitive
- RESERVED, do not use until Harsh says so: Malar on the terrace edge, the suicide attempt, "Papa, I am coming", Sagar pulling
  her back (Ch 35-37). The strongest beat in the book and the most likely to get an ad restricted. `lint_script.py` refuses it.
- Sensitive, handle with care: the drugging and confinement of Harsh (say "locked him up before the wedding", no drug detail in
  a hook); Malar's body and illness (never mock; reframe, as the book does); the sex scene in Ch 35 (never in an ad).
- Caste/samaj: describe the pressure as the book does; do not name a real community.
