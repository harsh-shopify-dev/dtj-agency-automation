# Creators: unique, real-looking people (never the "AI face")

Harsh, 21 Oct 2026, with 7 reference reels of real Indian book creators: "the image needed work, it looked like AI ... look at
the setup, their clothes, facial structures and skin tones (all different and unique)."

## What the references have that AI defaults miss
- **Faces**: different shapes (round, long, square jaw, soft chin), real noses, natural brows, glasses on some, uneven skin with
  texture, shine on the T-zone, under-eye shadows, the odd blemish. Ages from about 20 to 35.
- **Skin tones**: dusky, wheatish, light-brown, fair, East Asian; never all the same.
- **Clothes with a personality**: block-print or embroidered kurtas, a cami with jeans, an off-shoulder top, an oversized
  cardigan, a tank top; jhumkas, bangles, a nose pin, a hair clip, a watch.
- **Sets that are their own**: a packed, uneven bookshelf (books lying sideways, plants, fairy lights, a framed poster), a warm
  lamp, a curtain, a bed or a desk; one creator outdoors by a railing with greenery.
- **Props**: a stack of books, a mic held like a podcaster, a plate of bangles, an open book fanning its pages.
- **Overlays**: a white rounded box with black hook text at the top, a small coloured sub-line; casual, not branded.

The last book character (book-01) read as AI because of: the generic fair, symmetrical "AI Indian girl" face; a bare white room;
flat blown-out window light; a plain lavender tee with nothing personal; skin too even.

## Rules for a new creator
1. Pick a persona below that has not been used (see the log), or write a new one with the same level of detail. Change at least
   four of: age, skin tone, face shape, hair, glasses/accessories, clothes, set, light.
2. Describe a real person, not a model: say what is imperfect (pores, a blemish, frizz, a crooked smile, a slight double chin).
3. Never describe the face of a real creator from a reference; take the setup's idea, not the person.
4. One persona = one setup for the whole ad (house rule 1).
5. Make the character in a fresh chat (ChatGPT image) or Flow's Nano Banana, from text only. Then put the real book in her hands
   in a second fresh chat with the cover photos (`assets/cover_front.jpg`), so the cover is the real one.
