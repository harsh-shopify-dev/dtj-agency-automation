# G1 Plan (remake) or script (UGC) before any picture or credit is spent (100 points)

Remake plan (plan.md, cut.json): every reference shot has a row with time, what is in it, our picture, still or Flow clip.
UGC script (script.json): runs `reel.sh slop` (playbook tells, policy) and `job lint`; read `watch/g1.json`.

| # | Check | Points |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Copies the reference shot for shot (remake: all cut times, same order, same look per shot; UGC: the chosen template's beats) | 20 |
| 2 | Hook lands in 3 s (UGC: 3-12 words in the opening phrase, first word at 0.0 s; remake: the reference's first shot kept) | 15 |
| 3 | Claims only from the approved facts; no "100 %", no cure/body outcomes, no AI person as a customer (slop.py policy = 0 hits) | 20 |
| 4 | Fits the audience's playbook (`audiences/<slug>.md`: length, pace, hook style, language mix, tone, trends; native, not translated; ingredient lines say their job; Gujarati/Hindi as the brief says) | 15 |
| 5 | Motion only where real (Flow for pour/flame/hand; graphic shots stay stills); clips 8 s default, 6 s minimum, never 4 s | 10 |
| 6 | Product visible, end card with the brand's own logo, no burned-in AI line (added at posting) | 10 |
| 7 | Length and pace match the reference (cuts per 10 s, total length within 10 %) | 10 |

Blocking: any slop.py policy hit; a claim not in the facts; 4 s clips; missing reference shots; another brand's name or logo
planned anywhere. Ideas: a sharper hook, a variant worth A/B testing (hook-only variants are cheap), a regional version.
