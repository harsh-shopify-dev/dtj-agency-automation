# G0 References: which reels to copy (100 points per candidate)

Input per candidate: source (Meta Ad Library / Instagram / Pinterest), brand, days running, number of variations or ads using
the same video, views / likes / comments (and the account's followers when known), the video or its 4 fps sheet + transcript,
and the client's brief (product, category, price tier, audience: city/region, language, age, gender if given).

| # | Check | Points | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Proven: running long | 20 | Ad Library: 30+ days = 15, 60+ = 20, under 14 = 0 (still testing). Organic: views per follower >= 3x = 15, >= 10x = 20 |
| 2 | Proven: reused | 10 | same creative in several ads / versions, or the brand re-posted the format: 10; once: 0 |
| 3 | Hook in the first 3 s | 15 | first word/visual change by 0.5 s, a question / surprise / bold visual, text readable in the safe zone |
| 4 | Audience fit | 20 | judged against the audience's playbook (`audiences/<slug>.md`: what holds them, trends, script style); same or close category and price tier; the language/region of the client's buyers (Gujarati buyers: Gujarati or Hindi works, English-only = -10); visuals the audience lives (Indian kitchen, festival, family) |
| 5 | We can make it | 15 | stills + edit, or Flow clips of simple action (pour, flame, hand), or an AI presenter: 15; needs real people talking as customers, celebrities, complex crowd scenes: 0 (and see claim risk) |
| 6 | Product is the hero | 10 | the pack or the dish is on screen most of the time, a clear end card |
| 7 | Edit worth learning | 10 | measurable edit grammar (zoom-blur cuts, mirror shrinks, beat-synced hits) or a script structure we do not have yet |

Blocking (never shortlist): needs a fake testimonial / AI person speaking as a customer; health or "100 %" claims we cannot
make; another brand's footage would have to be reused (we copy the format only); copyrighted music we have no right to
(note it: music needs Harsh's file).
Output: rank the candidates by score, top 5 with one line why and what we would copy; ideas: missing angles (a regional
version, a festival in the next 30 days, a format the client's competitors use that the watchlist does not cover).
