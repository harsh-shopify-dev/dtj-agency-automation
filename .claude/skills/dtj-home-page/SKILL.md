# DTJ home page: only when asked, planned per brand

Harsh, 13 Oct 2026:
- "it should only design the homepage when told, like I should have options that what needs to be updated"
- "the AI will decide itself according to the brand what's needed and what not ... there should be a must part ... if anything can add
  more impact it should add that section or else leave if it just makes something repetitive"
- "99% we will work on multi product page stores"

What that means here:
- **Never on its own.** The bot and the product-page flow never touch a home page. A home page is built only when a person asks in the
  session.
- **The plan decides, the person can overrule.** "Design the home page" builds the plan (`--sections auto`). "Update the hero" or "add
  press logos" builds only those sections (`--sections hero` / `press`), and everything else on the page stays exactly as it is,
  hand edits in the theme editor included.
- **Draft theme only.** Under Harsh's standing rule (CLAUDE.md rule 2, home pages added 13 Oct 2026), a home page that passes every
  gate goes to the DRAFT theme without asking. The live theme stays out of reach.

## How the plan decides (`page_builder/home_plan.py`, rules in `recipes/home.json` "plan")
Every section gets one decision, always with its reason:

| Decision | Meaning |
|---|---|
| MUST | the page needs it for this kind of store; built whenever its facts exist |
| NEEDS FACTS | a must whose facts are missing: a gap to fill (ask the client), never invented |
| ADD | not needed, but it adds something the page does not already say: proof, a new way to shop, the brand's voice |
| SKIP | it would repeat another section, it is not for this kind of store, its facts are missing, or the page is long enough |

| | Multi-product store (default) | Single-product store (`"store_type": "single_product"`) |
|---|---|---|
| Must | hero, trust, categories, best-sellers, reasons, faq | hero, trust, reasons, faq |
| Add-ons, strongest first | press, before-after, videos, concerns, announcement, compare, story, wave | before-after, videos, press, compare, story, announcement |
| Never | (none) | categories, best-sellers, concerns, wave (one product: nothing to browse; the hero's button leads to it), and the theme's own product row |
| Length | 11 sections | 9 sections |

Thin strips (announcement, trust) count half; the wave counts nothing. Repetition is skipped:
- concerns that are mostly the same collections as the categories;
- a top strip that mostly says what the trust strip says;
- a wave with no shop-by-concern band below it.

**Your judgement on top.** Read the plan against the brand before building. You may swap an ADD or SKIP section in or out when the
brand's own facts make a clear case (for example, a heritage brand whose story is its strongest asset). In that case, name the sections
yourself and say why in one line. Never drop a MUST that has its facts. Never add a section whose facts are missing.

## Colour and the hero (fixed, Harsh 19 Oct 2026: "we will keep the same for future and only copy and colors will be changed")
- Colours: the palette method (`brand.json`: `"palette": "ramps"`, `colors.primary` + `colors.accent` measured from the pack or the

## Design rules (measured on 23 reference home pages, 19 Oct 2026)
Harsh, 19 Oct 2026: our home page "looks cramped and flat". The rules below come from 23 public home pages measured by code and read
from their screenshots: `references/study/<brand>.md` (one note each), `references/study-comparison.md` (generated by
`scripts/study_compare.py` from `scripts/study_numbers.py`), `references/study-findings.md` (what they say, where ratings.md was wrong).
"TOP" = the 6 pages Harsh rated 85+. The targets live in `services/store-builder/page_builder/home_profiles.json` (per category; spacing
is the same for all, the other ranges are guidance and several categories rest on one or two pages, marked `confidence: low`).

| Rule | Measured (TOP pages) | What the builder does | Checked by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Space between a section's edge and its first / last words | desktop 80-112 px (median 101), phone 40-66 (median 56); no single gap under 48 / 24. LOW pages: desktop median 39 | ONE rhythm on the theme's section wrapper (`styling.HOME_SPACE`): 80 px, which the theme itself makes 56 on phones (0.7 under 990 px); 48 (34) on the side a related neighbour shares (the shop row under the categories, the concern band under the wave); 0 for thin strips; the hero keeps its own look. The block's own padding is 0 and the wrapper carries the band colour | lint L20 (every build), `home_checks` HC1 and HC6 (measured in a browser) |
| Heading sizes | section headings 32-56 px desktop (median 42), 26-35 phone (median 32); H1 48-66 desktop, 30-40 phone; one size for every section heading | `styling.HOME_TYPE`: hero 56 / 36, section headings 40 / 30, the categories ring 28 / 22 (navigation, not a headline) | `home_checks` HC5 |
| Section order | see below | the recipe's order (`recipes/home.json`) | `home_checks` HC4 |
| Colour pacing | neighbours never share a fill; strong bands 0-3 per page; colour shares do not separate good from weak pages | the palette method (`palette.py`, `assemble_home.RAMPS_RHYTHM`), per brand | lint L04, L05; `home_checks` HC2; HC3 reports the strong bands, never fails on them |

**Section order, with reasons (each from the 23 pages; `home_profiles.json` "order"):**
1. The hero first: every page in the study opens with it.
2. A way to shop (categories, best sellers, concerns) within 2 sections under the hero: every TOP page does this; a multi-product shopper
   came to browse.
3. The first proof (press, customer videos, before / after) by three quarters of the page: on the TOP pages it sits at 0.14-0.73.
4. The brand story after the first way to shop: no page of the 23 tells its story first.
5. The FAQ, when there is one, within the last 2 sections: AG1 (the only TOP page with an FAQ) puts it second to last.
A fixed sequence beyond this ("categories, best sellers, proof strip, how it works, ...") is NOT supported: the TOP pages differ.

**Colour pacing (Harsh, 19 Oct 2026: "every brand has their own color palette and aesthetics"):** there is no fixed number of colour
bands. Each brand's palette decides how many strong bands it has and where; the references show placements to choose from, not a count:
- the loudest fill on ONE idea mid-page (Blueland: its one solid band is the reviews);
- colour inside cards, photos and buttons with no full-width band at all (Ritual);
- one hue in several strengths: white, a near-white, a pale tint, one strong fill (Blueland);
- colour in the cards (benefit, review, comparison and bundle cards) on a mostly white or cream ground, the loudest tint used twice with
  quieter sections between (Grubbly Farms: 0.72 of the page is ground);
- one cream ground with colour only in photos, small badges and the button (Branch Basics).
What never bends here: neighbouring sections never share a background (L04, HC2; the hero, thin strips and the wave sit on or between
bands by design) and the direction's dark-band limit (L05). Note for Harsh: two TOP pages do let white or cream neighbours touch and part
them with space alone (Ritual: one white run of 4,788 px; Branch Basics: 2,741 px of cream); L04 is kept as his rule until he decides.

**Check a build:** `cd services/store-builder` then `python -m page_builder.home_checks --run ../../.work/clients/<client>/home-run`
(template checks). To measure the spacing, render the page first (the elevate-home preview tool:
`python ../../.work/clients/elevate-home/preview_home.py <out> <out>/template.json`, it uses `render.home_page_html`) and add
`--html <out>/page.html`. Exit 0 = pass; the result is in `<run>/home-checks.json`.

## Rules that never bend (CLAUDE.md rule 5: never invent offers, prices, claims, reviews, press logos or comparisons)
| Section (option) | Built only from | Never |
|---|---|---|
| press | real mentions, each with its logo AND the article's address | a logo without an article |
| compare | rows the brand owner approved (`claims_approved: true`); an empty cell shows a tick or a cross | rows about other brands without approval |
| before-after | a real photo pair with the person's permission (`consent`: who and when) | staged or stock photos; stars (always off) |
| categories, concerns, best-sellers | the store's REAL collections (each tile and tab shows its collection's own name, link and picture) | a collection with no products; a "best sellers" heading the facts do not say |
| announcement | the store's own shipping, returns, payment or support facts; an offer only when it is the store's running offer | a made-up offer |
| hero, trust, reasons, story, videos, faq | the client's facts with their sources | a rating, review or number not in the facts |

A section whose facts are missing is left out, and the reason is recorded. Never fill a gap with invented words. Sample content (source
starting `SAMPLE`, or `demo: true` on our own dev store) is listed for replacement and goes only to a draft.

## Steps

### H0. Orient
1. Read `CLAUDE.md` and the newest entry of `docs/HANDOFF.md`.
2. Confirm the store's ORIGINAL `.myshopify.com` address and its draft theme id. dtj_stores holds both; on the test store,
   `elevate-with-harsh.myshopify.com` and draft `188893495469`.
3. The client folder is `.work/clients/<client>/` and holds `brand.json` (the same one as the product pages) and
   `home-facts.json` (the shape is in `services/store-builder/page_builder/schemas/home-facts.schema.json`).

### H1. Facts (read-only on the store)
- **Ask first: collect the brand's data?** (Harsh, 19 Oct 2026: "if i am starting the design of a product page or homepage it should
  ask me if it should run the scraper or not and using the data available it should start designing"). Ask the person before H1 goes
  on, unless they already said.
  - Yes: `cd services/store-builder` then `python -m page_builder.brand_intake <brand site> --client ../../.work/clients/intake-<host>`
    (add `--drive "DTJ Clients"` when the laptop has the DTJ_GDRIVE_* keys). It reads the public store once: every picture of at most
    20 products, the logo, collections, policies (missing or template text), fonts, the theme's colours, and a palette proposal
    (palette.py). One folder per website: product pages of the same brand use it too.
  - No: do not collect it again; use an earlier `.work/clients/intake-<host>/intake/` when there is one.
  - With an intake: read its BRAND.md first; confirm the brand colour, accent, category and price tier (they are guesses), then copy
    `intake/brand.json` to `<client>/brand.json`.
- **Store type:** `curl -s -A "Mozilla/5.0" https://<store>/products.json?limit=250`.
  - One product (its variants don't count): set `"store_type": "single_product"`.
  - Otherwise leave it out (multi-product is the default).
- **Collections:** list the store's real collections and check each has products:
  - `curl -s -A "Mozilla/5.0" https://<store>/collections.json?limit=250`
  - `curl -s -A "Mozilla/5.0" https://<store>/collections/<handle>/products.json?limit=1`

  Use only collections with products for categories, concerns and best sellers.
- **Store data through the Shopify CLI only** (Harsh, 14 Oct 2026), never the Shopify connector:
  - Files: `shopify store execute -s <store> -j -q '{ files(first: 250) { nodes { ... on MediaImage { image { url width height } } ... on Video { filename } } } }'`
    (a wide photo with no printed words for each banner; vertical videos for the customer cards).
  - Missing collections or other store changes need a person's yes, and auto mode blocks `--allow-mutations`: give the person a
    script to run in their own PowerShell (example: `.work/clients/elevate-home/make_collections.py`, which creates, fills and
    publishes collections and finds the CLI inside the Claude app's folder).
- **Pictures:** everything else comes from the client: their own site, their files, what they send. Each picture role in `media`
  must be a file already in the store's Files (`shopify://shop_images/<file>`).
- **The hero picture:** a multi-product store ALWAYS gets the full-image banner hero (Harsh, 14 Oct 2026: "for multi product page stores we will never use this hero section we will use the full image banner"). Pick a WIDE picture from the store's Files with an empty side for the words; a square pack shot puts the headline on the product. If the store has none, ask the client for one. Only a one-product shop may set `"layout": "split"` in `hero`. With 2-3 good banner photos, add `hero.slides` for a slideshow hero: banner 1 carries the main message (most shoppers never wait for banner 2); banners 2-3 point to a category each.
- **A separate phone banner for every slide** (Harsh, 26 Oct 2026: "generate banner images separately for mobile version"): on phones
  the words sit on the LOWER part of the picture (`mobile_layout: overlay`), so each slide gets its own tall picture
  (`image_mobile_role`; picture kind `banner_mobile`, 1024 x 1536): the subject in the upper half, the lower half a calm plain area
  for the headline and button. Never a square or a wide picture with the subject low: the words would land on the product.
- **What can't be collected:** write down what is missing; don't invent it.

### H2. The plan: show it in plain words
`cd services/store-builder` then `python -m page_builder.ship_home --client ../../.work/clients/<client> --list`

1. Tell the person what the page will have and why, in one line per section, from the printed reasons.
2. Name the NEEDS FACTS gaps and exactly which facts would unlock each one (for example, "3 collections with products for the
   category rings").
3. Apply your judgement (above). If the person asked for specific sections, those are the sections. Don't re-plan the page.
4. Pick the mode:
   - "design / redo the home page": `--fresh` (the old page is backed up first);
   - "update / add <section>": no `--fresh`, so only that section changes.

   Ask only if the request doesn't say.

### H3. Build and write the draft
`python -m page_builder.ship_home --client <dir> --sections auto|<a,b,c> --store <store> --theme <draft id> [--fresh] [--client-registry page_builder/clients/<store>.json]`
- **What it does:**
  1. Builds the page as it will really be, so bands, colours and the wave fit it.
  2. Checks the theme is a DRAFT Horizon theme.
  3. Backs up its home page.
  4. Merges the sections into it, or replaces the page with `--fresh`.
  5. Gates: lint errors 0, build problems 0.
  6. Writes `templates/index.json` and reads it back.
- **What it refuses:**
  - a live theme;
  - any file other than the home page;
  - a home page edited in the theme editor after the merge. Run it again in that case, so the edit is kept.
- **"needs a look":** fix what it names (usually a fact) and run again.
- **A client's own app copies:** pass `--client-registry`.
- **Result:** `<client>/home-run/ship-result.json` records the sections, changes, plan gaps (`gaps`) and samples. The action-log row
  records who approved it: the standing rule.

### H4. Check the real page
First the design checks on the build (see "Design rules": `python -m page_builder.home_checks --run <client>/home-run`, with
`--html` after a local render to measure the spacing). Then open `https://<store>/?preview_theme_id=<draft id>`. Check every changed
section on desktop and phone: it shows, there's no sideways scroll, the words read clearly against their background, and each band's
colour runs from edge to edge (the space now sits on the theme's section wrapper, which carries the band colour).
Add Harsh's verdict to the calibration log.

How to check:
- Use the built-in browser and scroll through, because sections fade in on scroll.
- Measure sideways scroll in the page itself (document width = window width at 375 px).
- A plain headless `--screenshot` misleads: it lays the page out wider than a phone, and lazy parts stay empty.

Known limits:
- Best Sellers tabs and the concern dropdown appear only with 2 or more collections that have products.
- Ratings appear only from real reviews (Judge.me).

### H5. Report and record
- **To the person, in plain words:**
  - which sections are on the page and why;
  - the gaps (NEEDS FACTS) and what would fill them;
  - what changed;
  - the samples to replace;
  - the preview link.
- **In the project:** add a `docs/HANDOFF.md` entry.

## Options reference
| Option | Block | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| announcement | DTJ Commerce marquee-band (bold) | top strip; icons follow the words (truck, return, shield) |
| hero | DTJ Heroes hero-banner, or DTJ Sections 2 slideshow-pro | multi-product: the full-image banner (by niche); with `hero.slides` (1-2 more banners, each with its own picture, headline and button) it becomes an edge-to-edge slideshow of 2-3 banners, banner 1 = the hero's main message. Banner pictures: photos WITHOUT printed words (the phone crops the same picture to 4:5). `hero.layout: "split"` only for one-product shops |
| trust | DTJ Commerce marquee-band (line) | 3+ trust points |
| press | DTJ Commerce press-strip | 3+ mentions with articles |
| categories | circle-menu (beauty, fashion, wellness), category-grid (home, general), category-slider (fashion, food) | 3+ collections. The grid shows 3 a row on desktop, so it takes 3 or 5 tiles (bento at 5); 4 go to the next block. The menu swipes in one row on phones from 4 tiles, so no tile sits alone, and its heading stays small (28 px desktop, 22 px phone): it is a way to move around, not a headline |
| best-sellers | DTJ Commerce best-sellers | one tab per collection (list 2+ collections in `best_sellers.collections` so the shopper can switch categories); replaces the theme's own product row |
| reasons | DTJ Heroes feature-cards / feature-tiles | by niche |
| before-after | DTJ Shop before-after | consent required, stars off |
| compare | DTJ Heroes compare-showcase | owner approval required |
| story | DTJ Heroes hero-banner (split) | the words follow the panel's colour (lint L01 checks it) |
| wave | DTJ Commerce wave-divider | colours from its neighbours; left out when they match or nothing follows |
| concerns | DTJ Shop concern-slider | 2+ collections |
| videos | DTJ Heroes video-reviews | 3+ real customer cards with permission |
| faq | DTJ Sections faq | 3+ questions |
