## For a multi-product Indian home-care store (blue and green, never yellow or brown)
Copy (as a rule):
- Next to "shop by category", add "shop by problem" (for home care: stains, smell, germs, hard water), each a photo tile with a short label.
- On every product card: one line naming the problem it solves, the star rating, price with strike price, size choices as small pills, and a full-width add-to-cart.
- Review cards that name the product reviewed and show a verified-buyer mark and a date, from the client's real review app only.
- A short brand statement section (one heading, one line) with the most space on the page (here 140 px top and bottom), followed by four value icons with one line each.
- Inset banners are fine, but give alternate sections a pale blue or green ground, so the page has a rhythm.

Avoid:
- A page that is white from top to bottom (here one white band of 6576 px): nothing separates offer, products and proof.
- Banners with the words inside the picture (sections 3 and 4): they cannot be read by search or edited, and the phone needs a second picture.
- A cookie panel that covers the first product row.
- Reviews only near the bottom (10th of 11); move one proof section into the top half.
