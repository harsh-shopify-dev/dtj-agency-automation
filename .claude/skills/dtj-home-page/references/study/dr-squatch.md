## For a multi-product Indian home-care store (blue and green, never yellow or brown)
Copy (as a rule):
- One off-white ground for every other section (here about half the page, 0.51); put a photo or a tinted band between them, never two fills of the same kind side by side.
- Tabs above the product row so one row covers several product types (for home care: by room or task).
- Product card: badge, name, price, strike price, save %, stars with count, one line, a subscribe-and-save choice when the client offers it, a full-width buy button.
- A row of portrait customer videos after the benefits, separate from a written-review carousel further down.
- If the client sells refills or repeat orders, one short subscription section: 3 icons, 3 plain lines, one button.
- One control for every carousel (a progress line and two arrows), and one solid accent for every main button (12px corners here).

Avoid:
- A pop-up whose shade sits over the first screen on arrival.
- A contest or celebrity campaign banner high on the page (3rd here), between products and categories.
- A seasonal free-gift hero plus a quiz discount plus a subscription offer on one page: three promotions compete.
- Wood-grain textures and brown panels: the brief for home care is blue and green, never brown.
- Crude jokes or swear words in benefit copy; Indian family shoppers read them as careless.
