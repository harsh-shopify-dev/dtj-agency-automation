# Home page study: what the 23 pages say, and where ratings.md is wrong (19 Oct 2026)

Read with `study-comparison.md` (generated by `scripts/study_compare.py`; every figure below is from it or from a brand note in `study/`).
TOP = the 6 pages Harsh rated 85+ (Blueland, Branch Basics, AG1, Grubbly Farms, Ritual, Method), MIDDLE = 75-84, LOW = 74 and under.
Ratings are Harsh's judgement from screenshots, not conversion data. Wakefit (desktop capture blocked) and section-store-home-1 are not part
of the study.

## What separates the good pages (measured)
1. **Spacing is the clearest signal.** Desktop pad (the empty space between a section's edge and its first or last words): TOP 80-112 px
   (median 101), MIDDLE 52-98 (median 62), LOW 5-86 (median 39). Phone: TOP 40-66 (median 56), MIDDLE 34-58 (median 53.5), LOW 2-40
   (median 28). On a phone the good and middle pages look alike; the low ones are cramped.
2. **One heading size per page.** TOP section headings 32-56 px on desktop (median 42) and 26-35 on phones (median 32); H1 48-66 desktop.
   The good pages use the same size for every section heading (Blueland 40 x 10, AG1 56); several LOW pages jump between sizes (G FUEL
   43 to 78 on one page).
3. **Shop first, story later.** On every TOP page a way to shop (categories, best sellers or a bundle) is the first or second section under
   the hero. No page of the 23 tells its brand story before a way to shop.
4. **Proof is spread out.** The first proof (press, reviews, customer photos or a stat) sits between 0.14 (Branch Basics) and 0.73
   (Grubbly Farms) of the page on TOP pages. Proof sections per TOP page: 1 (Grubbly Farms, Method) to 4 (Branch Basics, AG1); Blueland adds
   proof inside other sections too (a count in a heading, ratings on product cards, certification badges), each in its own format.
5. **Colour: placement, not a count.** Colour shares do not separate the groups (ground share TOP 0.55-0.90, LOW 0.00-1.00; strong bands
   TOP 0-3). What the TOP pages share is WHERE colour goes: the loudest fill on one idea (Blueland: the reviews band), colour inside cards
   and photos instead of bands (Ritual: no full-width colour band at all), one hue family in several strengths (Blueland: white, a near-white
   blue, a pale tint and one strong blue), or colour carried by cards on a mostly white ground (Grubbly Farms). Each brand's palette
   decides the count (Harsh, 19 Oct 2026).
6. **White neighbours are not a fault on the good pages.** Ritual runs one white ground for 4,788 px over 11 sections and Branch Basics
   2,741 px of cream; they part sections with space and photo placement. Our rule L04 (neighbours never share a fill) is stricter than the
   TOP pages: kept as Harsh's rule, flagged for his decision.

## Where ratings.md is wrong, and why
- **Shared finding 2, "ONE solid brand band at most":** not a rule the data supports. TOP pages carry 0 (Branch Basics, Grubbly Farms,
  Ritual), 1 (Blueland, Method) or 3 (AG1) strong bands. It describes Blueland, not the good pages as a group.
- **Shared finding 3, the order "hero, categories, best sellers, proof strip, how it works, reviews, story, FAQ":** too specific. Only
  Blueland puts categories right under the hero among the TOP pages (Grubbly Farms and AG1 open with best sellers, Branch Basics with a
  press row, Ritual with a section its note marks "other", Method with an editorial row); how-it-works is on 2 of 6 TOP pages; an FAQ on 1 of 6 (AG1, second to
  last). What does hold: a way to shop within 2 sections, proof before three quarters of the page, the story after the shop.
- **Shared finding 4, "8-12 sections, 4,500-8,000 px; Mamaearth and AG1 the exceptions":** 3 of the 6 TOP pages have 8-12 sections
  (Blueland 11, Grubbly Farms 12, Method 8); Branch Basics has 7, Ritual 17 and AG1 19. 4 of 6 are 4,500-8,000 px long (Grubbly Farms 8,907,
  AG1 16,294). Ritual is a third exception the finding misses.
- **Shared finding 1, "84-112 px desktop":** the TOP range starts at 80 (Ritual). The phone range 40-66 holds once the nav bars that two
  captures started with are left out.
- **Whole Truth, "very airy" (152 / 129):** that figure is ONE measurement: the page is one block, not separate sections, so the space
  between its sections was never measured. "Products sit far down" is also not what the screenshots show: a product category row is the
  second section, right under the hero (see `study/whole-truth.md`).
- **KittySpout, 96 / 40:** its review widget reports impossible gaps (-5113 px and others); without them the page measures 98 / 50.
- **Method, 98 / 64:** the saved files give 88 / 64 on desktop; the difference is not explained by the files.
- **Cooking Guild 56 / 57 and boAt 5 / 2:** ratings.md is right; the first reading of the saved files was not (it counted the nav bar).
- **Dr. Squatch, "n/m":** right; the page is one block, so its spacing cannot be measured from the files.
- **Colour verdicts:** several shares in the measured data are misreads (photos on flat backdrops read as tints, gradients missed, footers
  counted; listed in `study-comparison.md`), so the colour judgements in ratings.md rest on the screenshots, not on the shares.

## What we changed in the builder (19 Oct 2026)
- One vertical rhythm on the theme's section wrapper: 80 px between a section's edge and its content on desktop (the theme makes it 56 on
  phones), 48 px (34) on the side a related neighbour shares (the shop row under the categories, the concern band under the wave), 0 for
  thin strips; the hero keeps its own look. Measured on the test store's page in the local preview: pad 46 -> 102 px desktop, 30 -> 56 px
  phone (TOP range 80-112 / 40-66).
- One heading scale: hero 56 / 36 px, section headings 40 / 30, the categories ring 28 / 22 (it is navigation). Before, the same page used
  28 to 64 px.
- Checks: lint L20 (the rhythm) gates every home build; `page_builder/home_checks.py` checks rhythm, neighbouring colours, order, type and
  the measured spacing, and reports the strong bands without failing on them.
